# VextoraTech — Services

VextoraTech covers the full product lifecycle — strategy, design, development, AI integration, and cloud deployment.

## AI & Machine Learning

We design and ship production AI systems — not demos. Typical work includes:

- Computer vision: object detection, OCR, ANPR, face recognition
- Voice AI: Whisper transcription, diarization, and call analytics
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with vector search
- Local inference stacks (Ollama, GGUF) for cost-controlled deployments
- Model integration via OpenAI-compatible and open-weight APIs

Deliverables often include model pipelines, evaluation harnesses, APIs that expose inference, and monitoring for production accuracy and latency.

## Computer Vision

Specialized vision systems for real-world operations: attendance via facial recognition, multi-camera surveillance with anomaly alerts, ANPR, OCR, and object detection using stacks such as YOLO, OpenCV, and PyTorch.

## Voice AI / Contact-center analytics

End-to-end voice pipelines that transcribe calls, separate speakers (diarization), extract sentiment and topics, and surface action items — useful for contact centers and sales teams.

## RAG & LLM applications

Grounded assistants trained on your documents and knowledge bases. We build chunking, embeddings, retrieval, citations, and chat UIs so answers stay factual and auditable.

## Engagement models

### Fixed-scope build
A defined MVP or feature set with agreed deliverables, timeline, and acceptance criteria. Best when requirements are clear.

### Ongoing product partnership
A retained engineering team embedded in your roadmap — sprints, demos, continuous delivery. Best for products in active growth.

### Discovery / PoC
A short engagement to validate feasibility — architecture review, prototype, or technical spike — before a full build.
