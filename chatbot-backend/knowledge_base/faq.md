# VextoraTech — FAQ

## What does VextoraTech do?

VextoraTech builds AI-powered software and full-stack digital products for startups and enterprises — computer vision, voice AI, RAG/LLM apps, web, mobile, APIs, UI/UX, and cloud/DevOps.

## Where is VextoraTech based?

Lahore, Pakistan. The team is remote-friendly across Pakistan.

## When was the company founded?

VextoraTech was incorporated in 2026.

## What industries do you serve?

We work with founders and teams across operations, government/IoT portals, hospitality, healthcare knowledge tools, field operations, and support/contact-center products. If you have a product problem that needs strong engineering (especially with an AI angle), we are a fit to talk.

## Do you only do AI projects?

No. AI is a core strength, but we also ship full-stack web platforms, mobile apps, APIs, design systems, and cloud infrastructure — often with AI features integrated where they create real value.

## How do engagements work?

Three common models: fixed-scope builds, ongoing product partnerships, and short discovery/PoC engagements. See the process and services knowledge for details.

## How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A focused PoC can be weeks; an MVP is often measured in months. We define timeline during discovery once requirements and constraints are clear. We do not invent fixed timelines without scoping.

## Do you share pricing publicly?

Pricing depends on scope, team composition, and engagement model. Contact info@vextoratech.com or https://vextoratech.com/contact for an honest assessment — we do not publish a single public price list.

## Can we talk directly to engineers?

Yes. Positioning is "built by engineers, for founders" — clients work with the people doing the work, not a sales layer in between.

## Where can I see your work?

https://vextoratech.com/projects — case studies across inventory, IoT, field tracking, AI helpdesk, vision, medical assistants, and voice analytics.
