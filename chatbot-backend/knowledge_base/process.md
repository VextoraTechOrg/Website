# VextoraTech — How We Work

## Delivery process

### Discovery
We deep-dive into your goals, user needs, and technical constraints. Together we define scope, stack, timeline, and success metrics.

### Design
Wireframes evolve into high-fidelity Figma prototypes. User flows are tested before heavy coding begins.

### Build
Agile sprints with weekly demos. Full-stack development with code reviews, automated tests, and real deployment from early sprints.

### Launch & scale
Production deployment, performance monitoring, and ongoing support. We do not disappear after go-live.

## Engagement models (recap)

- **Fixed-scope build** — defined MVP/features, agreed deliverables and acceptance criteria.
- **Ongoing product partnership** — retained team embedded in your roadmap.
- **Discovery / PoC** — short spike to validate feasibility before a full build.
