# VextoraTech — Projects & Case Studies

Some case studies were delivered by our people before incorporation; some are built under VextoraTech. Same engineers, same standards — under one name from 2026 onward. Full portfolio: https://vextoratech.com/projects

## QClose Inventory

Inventory management dashboard with hardware scanner integration for product addition and retrieval, plus reporting for opening and closing stock levels.

## SWGNP

IoT-based web portal for remote sensing devices used by government stakeholders, with advanced search and dynamic data visualization.

## PYLI

Centralized platform for managing multiple business profiles with a customized UI tailored to client requirements.

## Restaurant Management System

Full-featured RMS with menu customization, orders, bookings, and billing — including frontend leadership on a new product version.

## NexaWatch

Real-time field visibility platform for teams, routes, and client visits — live GPS tracking, geofences, route history, and secure admin access in one dashboard.

## NexaDesk AI

Secure AI helpdesk for modern support teams — smarter ticket analysis, context-aware replies, priority detection, and knowledge suggestions in one dashboard.

## Facial Recognition Attendance System

Automated employee attendance using real-time facial recognition. Detects and identifies faces from live camera feeds, logs check-ins and check-outs, and generates attendance reports.

## AI Surveillance System

Intelligent video surveillance with real-time object and anomaly detection across multiple camera feeds. Triggers alerts for restricted zone breaches, loitering, and suspicious activity.

## Medical Knowledge Assistant

AI assistant grounded on medical literature and clinical guidelines, enabling professionals to retrieve drug information, diagnostic criteria, and treatment protocols through conversational queries.

## MedAssist AI

Patient-facing medical chatbot that triages symptoms, answers health queries, and guides users to appropriate care pathways, with handoff to appointment systems.

## Voice Intelligence Hub

End-to-end voice analytics platform that transcribes, diarizes, and analyzes call recordings. Extracts sentiment, key topics, and action items for contact centers and sales teams.
