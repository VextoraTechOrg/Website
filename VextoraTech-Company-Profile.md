### 4.3 Mobile App Development

### 4.4 Cloud & DevOps

## 7. How we work (delivery process)

1. **Discovery** — Goals, user needs, constraints; define scope, stack, timeline, and success metrics.
2. **Design** — Wireframes → high-fidelity Figma prototypes; flows tested before heavy coding.
3. **Build** — Agile sprints, weekly demos, code reviews, tests, and real deployment from early sprints.
4. **Launch & scale** — Production deployment, monitoring, and ongoing support after go-live.
